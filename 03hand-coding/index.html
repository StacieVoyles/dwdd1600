Custom Crochet Blankets Made Your Way

Choose Your Colors

Select yarn colors that match your style, room, or gift recipient. Your blanket can be soft and neutral, bright and playful, or anything in between.

Pick Your Patterns

Mix and match crochet patterns to create a blanket that feels completely personal. Each section can have its own texture and design.

Make It Meaningful

Every blanket is handmade and designed around your choices. Your combination is what makes it one of a kind.

Whether you are creating a blanket for yourself or giving one as a gift, Mix + Match Custom Crochet lets you be part of the design process.

Blanket Options

  • Choose your favorite colors
  • Select your preferred crochet patterns

How It Works

  1. Choose your blanket size.
  2. Select your favorite colors.
  3. Pick the crochet patterns you love.
  4. Submit your choices for your custom blanket.

Crochet Terms

Yarn
The material used to crochet each custom blanket.
Pattern
The stitch design that creates the texture and look of the blanket.
Border
The finished edge added around the outside of the blanket.
