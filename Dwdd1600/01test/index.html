Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet in a safe, responsible, respectful, and informed way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the laws and rules that govern how people use technology, digital information, and the internet.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules explaining how students or employees are allowed to use an organization’s computers, internet, devices, and online accounts.

What is Plagarisim?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any dishonest action used to gain an unfair advantage in schoolwork.

What are the rules for using AI generated content?

AI-generated content should only be used when allowed, properly credited, carefully checked for accuracy, and not presented dishonestly as your own work.
